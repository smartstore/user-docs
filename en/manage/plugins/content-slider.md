# Content Slider

A content slider is ideally suited to convey important information to the visitors of a page. Visitors' eyes are directed straight to this area during their first visit. Therefore, you should place information there that is of particular interest to your shop visitors (e.g., special offers). You can use the content slider for slides on the homepage and on [category pages](../../manage/catalog/organizing-product-categories.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
After you have installed the Content Slider plugin, you must activate the Content Slider widget under **CMS > Widgets**.
{% endhint %}

## How to Configure the Content Slider

You can configure the content slider for the homepage in the administration area under **Plugins > Content Slider**. You can configure the slides for category pages directly on the [category detail pages](../../manage/catalog/organizing-product-categories.md) on the **Content Slider** tab.

Here you can enter the height and the background image and define whether the content should slide in automatically without user interaction.

## How to Configure a Single Slide

When adding or editing a single slide, you can define a **Title**, **Text**, and an **Image**, and configure multiple buttons that change automatically with the content of the slide (see graphic below). You must also set the language for the slide. There are no localizations for individual slides. Therefore, you must set up a slide for every active language in your shop if you want to display the same content in different languages. You can add up to three buttons to each slide. To add a button to a slide, you simply have to activate it by checking **Published**. Then enter the button **Text** and an absolute URL including the protocol (e.g.: [http://www.myshop.com/mypath](http://www.myshop.com/mypath)) and select the **Button type**. Possible **Button types** are _Primary, Info, Success, Warning, Danger, Inverse_, and _Link_.

{% hint style="info" %}
The **URL** of a button can be specified either completely (including _http_ or _https_ protocol) or absolutely (e.g. ~~_/path/to/resource_), whereby the preceding tilde (~~) can also be omitted.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
The colors of the button types are defined in the configuration area of your theme. For further information on configuring themes, please read [Working with Themes](../configuration/working-with-themes.md).
{% endhint %}
